Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2026-09-13

Welcome to Mindful Space. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how information is collected, used, and stored when you use our application.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: If you register or sign in, we process your chosen username and a cryptographic hash of your password generated locally using PBKDF2/SHA-256. We never collect or store plain-text passwords.
  • Practice & Usage Data: Session counts, streak history, minutes meditated, and weekly metrics are tracked to provide personalized analytics.
  • Local Cache & Preferences: User settings (e.g., volume control and theme states) are stored locally in your browser storage.

2. How Your Data Is Stored

  • Local Storage: Your activity metrics are primarily saved on your local device using standard browser storage APIs.
  • Cloud Synchronization: When authenticated, progress data may be synchronized to our secure cloud database (e.g., MongoDB Atlas / Google Cloud) solely for cross-device backup and progression tracking.

3. Third-Party Services & Audio

This application does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Audio playback and breathing exercises run locally within your browser environment.

4. Service Workers & Caching

We utilize Progressive Web App (PWA) technologies, including Service Workers, to cache application shell files and audio assets locally on your device for offline functionality.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can clear your local practice data at any time by clearing your browser cache and local storage. To request deletion of synchronized cloud account data, please contact the developer.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via our GitHub repository or project contact channel.
